A letter for you

My dearest Ninette,

Another year of you in the world means another year the world got a little brighter. I wanted to build you something small and a little silly, because you deserve to be celebrated in every way I can think of — even in code.

I'm so proud of you, and of us, and of everything we've quietly built together. This year we bought our flat, and between all the holidays we've chased across the map, that's still the thing I'm proudest of — because wherever we go, I'm always just glad to be coming home to you.

You're on my mind more than you probably know. You're the reason for my mood most days, whether I say it out loud or not, and you put up with more of my nonsense than anyone reasonably should — and somehow still look at me like I'm the lucky one. You are, quite simply, beautiful, today and every day.

Here's to another year of us. I love you more than I know how to say, so I built you a website instead.

Yours, always
