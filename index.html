A letter for you

My dearest Ninette,

Another year of you in the world means another year the world got a little brighter. I wanted to build you something small and a little silly, because you deserve to be celebrated in every way I can think of — even in code.

I'm so proud of you, and of us, and of everything we've quietly built together. This year we bought our flat, and even though we didn't get away on many holidays this time round, we've had years of them before — and wherever we've gone, I've always just been glad to be coming home to you.

You're on my mind more than you probably know. You're the reason I wake up in the morning, whether I say it out loud or not, and you put up with more of my bullshit than anyone reasonably should — and somehow you still don't believe me when I say I'm so lucky to have you. You are light in darkness, the Ying to my yang, and my forever partner in crime.

I love you more than words can describe. But I tried to build you something that attempts to do the impossible: profess how much I love you and what you mean to me.

Yours, Daniel
