A letter for you

To my darling Ninette, happy 31st birthday.

I'm so proud of you, and of us, and of everything we've quietly built together. This year we bought our flat, and even though we didn't get away on many holidays we still got to experience Armenia together for the first time. But the one thing I loved about this year is being able to come home to you.

From the moment I wake up until I fall asleep, you are on my mind. You're the reason I wake up in the morning, the main reason I smile, and yet you put up with more of my bullshit than anyone reasonably should — and somehow you still don't believe me when I say I'm so lucky to have you. You are my light in darkness, the Ying to my yang, and my forever partner in crime.

I love you more than words can describe. But I tried to build you something that attempts to do the impossible: profess how much I love you and what you mean to me.

Yours, Daniel
